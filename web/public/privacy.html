Privacy Policy

Tactile Noise (software.tactile.noise) · Last updated: October 2026
Summary: Tactile Noise is an offline-first ambient sound generator. It does not collect, record, track, transmit, or share any personal information or analytics. All audio is synthesized directly on your device.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

We believe that accessibility and sleep tools should respect your privacy completely. When you install and use Tactile Noise:

  • No Personal Data: We do not ask for, collect, or store names, email addresses, phone numbers, or account credentials.
  • No Telemetry or Analytics: We do not integrate third-party analytics (such as Google Analytics or Firebase), tracking pixels, or diagnostic beacons.
  • No Advertising: There are no advertising SDKs, ad networks, or user tracking mechanisms in the app.
  • No Audio Recording: The app synthesizes sound using Web Audio and procedural digital signal processing (DSP); it never accesses your microphone or records ambient sound.

2. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

The app saves your user preferences (such as volume level, selected sound, favorite sound presets, tone/warmth adjustments, and sleep timer settings) locally on your device via standard browser local storage. This data never leaves your device and is deleted if you clear the app's storage or uninstall the application.

3. Network Communications

Tactile Noise is designed to function entirely offline. Once installed, the application requires no internet connection to synthesize sounds or operate any feature.

4. Wellness & Medical Disclaimer

Tactile Noise provides ambient audio designed for relaxation, focus, and general sleep support. It is not a medical device, is not intended for clinical use, and does not provide diagnosis, treatment, or cure for tinnitus, insomnia, or any other medical condition.

5. Changes to This Policy

If this policy is updated in the future, the revised version will be published here with an updated "Last updated" date.

6. Contact

If you have questions regarding this privacy policy, please reach out via our repository or developer contact on the Google Play Store listing.
